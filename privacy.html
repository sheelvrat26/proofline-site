Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Proofline uses Google sign-in only on its own Gmail account (sheelvrat26@gmail.com), to send business emails and read the replies to them. It does not request access to anyone else's Google account.

What we access

Gmail send permission, to send emails we write; Gmail read permission, to read replies in those email threads.

How we use it

Email content is used only to manage our own business conversations. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

We keep only the minimum needed to track a conversation (sender, subject, a short excerpt). To ask for your data to be deleted, or for any question, email sheelvrat26@gmail.com.
